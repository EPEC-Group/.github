# EPEC Group

Welcome to the EPEC Group GitHub organization.

EPEC Group is an Australian specialist in renewable energy grid connections and high-voltage engineering. Founded in 2019, we help deliver reliable, compliant power system solutions for solar, battery energy storage, wind, and other complex energy projects.

## What we do

- **Grid connections and compliance** — supporting projects from connection strategy and studies through to registration and commercial operation
- **Electrical engineering** — power systems, protection, modelling, control tuning, and performance analysis
- **EPC delivery** — integrated delivery of substations and renewable energy infrastructure
- **Commissioning and testing** — high-voltage commissioning, SCADA, compliance testing, and system integration
